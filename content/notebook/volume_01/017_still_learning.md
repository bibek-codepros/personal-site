# Still Learning

People sometimes assume confidence means you've figured everything out.

I don't think that's true.

If anything, experience has made me more comfortable saying,

"I don't know."

Technology changes constantly.

People change.

Life changes.

So I try to keep learning.

Sometimes that means reading documentation.

Sometimes it's listening to someone younger than me.

Sometimes it's simply admitting I was wrong.

I hope I never reach a point where I believe there's nothing left to learn.

Curiosity has carried me this far.

I think it deserves to stay with me for the rest of my life.
