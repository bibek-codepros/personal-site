# Home

If you've read every note in this volume, thank you.

These pages were never written to prove anything.

They're simply moments that shaped me.

Some are joyful.

Some still hurt.

Some make me smile every time I remember them.

Together, they tell a story that no résumé ever could.

When I started building HOME, I didn't want another personal website.

I wanted a place that felt honest.

A place where someone could spend a little time, leave with a smile, and maybe feel a little less alone.

If even one person finds comfort, courage or curiosity somewhere in these pages, then HOME has already done more than I hoped.

This isn't the end of the notebook.

Life keeps moving.

New memories will arrive.

New lessons will appear.

New volumes will be written.

For now...

Welcome home.
