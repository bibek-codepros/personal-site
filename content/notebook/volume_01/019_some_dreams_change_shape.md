# Some Dreams Change Shape

For a long time, I thought losing my Army dream meant losing my direction.

Today, I see it differently.

That dream didn't disappear.

It changed shape.

The discipline I admired in the Army still influences how I work.

The responsibility I wanted to carry still matters to me.

The desire to contribute to something bigger than myself never left.

Only the uniform changed.

Life has taught me that dreams aren't always abandoned.

Sometimes they're translated into another language.

If you had told the younger version of me that I'd one day help build technology companies instead of wearing military stars, I probably wouldn't have believed you.

But I think he'd still be proud.

Because the purpose remained the same.

To keep growing.

To keep serving.

To keep becoming someone useful.

Some dreams end.

Others quietly evolve.
