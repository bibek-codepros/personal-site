# Story Style Guide

Project HOME

---

Every chapter is written as literature.

Not marketing.

Not biography.

Not LinkedIn.

Not a résumé.

---

The reader should feel like they are sitting beside Bibek while he quietly tells them a story.

---

The writing should feel:

Honest

Warm

Reflective

Simple

Patient

Human

---

Never exaggerate.

Never self-promote.

Never rush.

---

Every chapter follows this rhythm.

Reflection

↓

Story

↓

Realization

↓

Lesson

↓

Transition

---

Every chapter ends with hope.

Never with completion.

Because HOME is still being written.

---

One sentence is more valuable than one hundred impressive words.

Always choose truth over cleverness.

---

Write so slowly that the reader forgets they are reading.

They should feel like they are remembering something.

Even if they have never lived it.
