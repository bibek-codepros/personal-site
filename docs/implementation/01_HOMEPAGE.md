# Homepage Product Specification

Version: 1.0

Status:
Production

Priority:
Critical

---

# Objective

The homepage is the emotional front door of Project HOME.

Its purpose is NOT to explain Bibek's career.

Its purpose is to make visitors feel like they have stepped into someone's home.

The homepage should create curiosity.

Not answer every question.

If visitors want to know more after leaving the homepage,

the homepage has succeeded.

---

# Success Metric

Within the first 30 seconds, visitors should understand:

- This is not a portfolio.
- This is a personal journey.
- This website feels different.
- I want to keep reading.

---

# Primary Emotion

The homepage should create one feeling.

> "Welcome."

Everything else is secondary.

---

# SECTION 02

# OPENING LETTER

## Purpose

Transform curiosity into connection.

This is the first moment where visitors should feel they are reading
something personal rather than browsing a website.

The opening letter sets the emotional tone for the entire project.

---

## Design Goal

Imagine opening the first page of a beautifully written book.

That is the feeling.

---

## Layout

Single column.

Maximum content width:

720px

Generous whitespace above and below.

No illustrations.

No decorative elements.

Typography carries the entire section.

---

## Heading

Before We Begin...

---

## Body

This should read like a personal letter.

Example direction:

Welcome.

I'm genuinely happy you're here.

The internet has plenty of places where people list achievements,
share polished highlights,
or try to impress strangers.

I wanted to build something different.

This isn't a place where I tell you how successful I am.

It's a place where I share how I became the person I am today.

You'll find stories,
small victories,
moments that changed my perspective,
and people who believed in me before I fully believed in myself.

If you stay for a while,
I hope you leave knowing me a little better.

Welcome home.

---

## Reading Experience

Maximum line width:

70 characters.

Paragraph spacing:

32px

Reading should feel slow.

Comfortable.

Never rushed.

---

## Motion

Entire section fades in as it enters viewport.

Opacity

0 → 100

Translate Y

20px → 0px

Duration

500ms

Trigger only once.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

Visitor pauses.

Visitor begins reading naturally.

No visual distraction competes with the text.

---

## Heading

Sometimes life doesn't celebrate your progress with trophies.

Sometimes...

- First international journey.
- Singapore transit.
- Bali destination.
- The feeling of looking outside the airplane window.
- Quiet reflection.
- Gratitude.
- Looking back at the younger version of yourself.

Hotels.

Tourist attractions.

Luxury.

Travel itinerary.

This chapter is emotional.

Not geographical.
