# Becoming

Version: 1.0

Status:
Production

Priority:

---

# Purpose

This page exists to answer one question.

> "How did Bibek become who he is today?"

Not through achievements.

Not through titles.

Through experiences.

The Becoming page is the emotional core of Project HOME.

If the Homepage says,

"Welcome."

Then Becoming says,

"Sit down. Let me tell you my story."

---

# Success Criteria

When a visitor finishes reading Becoming, they should feel:

"I don't just know what Bibek does.

I understand how he became this person."

---

# Emotional Journey

Curiosity

↓

Empathy

↓

Growth

↓

Gratitude

↓

Reflection

↓

Hope

The visitor should never feel overwhelmed.

Each chapter should naturally lead into the next.

---

# Design Philosophy

This page is not a résumé.

It is not a timeline.

It is not a biography.

It is a narrative.

The visitor should feel like they are reading chapters from a book.

---

# Reading Time

Approximately

8–12 minutes.

That is intentional.

Do not shorten it.

People who stay deserve the full story.

# Chapter 03

The Place That Believed In Me

Fast page transitions.

# Definition of Done

The page is complete when:

✓ Visitors understand the person behind the title.

✓ Code Pros feels like gratitude, not promotion.

✓ Window Seat becomes memorable.

✓ Every chapter has a lesson.

✓ The page feels like a memoir.

✓ The visitor reaches the end wanting to know what comes next.
