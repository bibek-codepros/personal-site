# SPRINT 1.2 — Homepage Refinement

Status: Production
Priority: CRITICAL
Estimated Time: 2–4 Hours

---

# Objective

The homepage already communicates the idea of HOME.

This sprint transforms it into something memorable.

The goal is **not** to redesign the homepage.

The goal is to refine every interaction, sentence, spacing decision, and visual rhythm until the homepage feels intentional.

Imagine Apple reviewing the page before launch.

Every pixel should justify its existence.

---

# Creative Principle

HOME is NOT:

- a portfolio
- a landing page
- a company website
- a resume

HOME IS:

A digital memoir.

Someone should feel like they are entering another person's world.

---

# Primary Goals

Improve:

- storytelling
- emotional connection
- readability
- pacing
- immersion
- polish

without increasing complexity.

---

# Section 01

## Hero

Priority:
★★★★★

---

### Replace the Hero Copy

Avoid introducing Bibek through his role.

The hero should immediately create curiosity.

It should communicate philosophy before profession.

Examples of emotional direction:

"I didn't become the person I planned to be."

"Some dreams quietly end.
Others quietly begin."

The exact wording may differ,
but it should create curiosity instead of introducing credentials.

---

### Reduce visual noise

Hero should breathe.

Increase whitespace.

Reduce competing elements.

The eye should naturally travel:

Headline

↓

Subheadline

↓

Primary CTA

---

### Hero animation

Headline

fade-up

0.4s

Subheadline

0.6s

CTA

0.8s

Background motion

extremely subtle.

Never distracting.

# Section 02

## One Minute Story

Priority:
★★★★★

Create a brand-new homepage section.

Purpose:

Allow visitors to understand the entire story within one minute.

This section should summarize:

- childhood curiosity
- Army dream
- career redirection
- technology
- purpose

without feeling like a biography.

Target reading time:

45–60 seconds.

---

# Section 03

Priority:
★★★★★

Remove traditional timeline.

Instead create six emotional chapters.

Curiosity

↓

The Dream

↓

Starting Again

↓

Belonging

↓

Leadership

↓

Still Becoming

Not milestones.

---

# Section 04

## Reading Rhythm

Priority:
★★★★★

Never place large text blocks directly beside another large block.

Alternate rhythm.

Large section

↓

Breathing space

↓

Small quote

↓

Large section

↓

Image

↓

Whitespace

Reading should feel calm.

---

# Section 05

## Quote Block

Priority:
★★★★☆

Introduce one memorable quote.

Suggested placement:

Between Hero and One Minute Story.

Example direction:

"Life wasn't closing a door.

Typography:

Very large.

Centered.

Lots of whitespace.

---

# Section 06

## Motion

Priority:
★★★★☆

Every animation must have purpose.

Allowed:

fade

blur reveal

slow parallax

opacity

translateY

Avoid:

bounce

rotation

zoom

oversized motion

Motion should feel invisible.

---

# Section 08

## Typography

Priority:
★★★★★

Improve paragraph spacing.

Limit text width.

Reading should resemble a book.

Not documentation.

# Section 13

## Footer

Priority:
★★★★★

Replace standard footer.

Desired feeling:

The visitor is leaving someone's home.

Suggested direction:

Thank you for spending a little time here.

If my story reminded you of your own...

perhaps we'd enjoy a conversation someday.

The coffee is on me.

Keep links below.

Do not let links dominate.

---

# Section 14

## Performance

Priority:
★★★★★

Maintain Lighthouse performance.

Animations must not reduce FPS.

Avoid unnecessary JavaScript.

Images lazy loaded.

Fonts optimized.

---

# Section 15

## Accessibility

Priority:
★★★★★

Keyboard navigation.

Proper heading hierarchy.

Reduced motion support.

Focus states.

High contrast.

Screen reader friendly.

---

# Section 16

## Emotional Audit

After implementation, review the homepage and answer honestly:

Does it feel like a website?

Or does it feel like entering someone's world?

If it still feels like a website...

continue refining.

---

# Final Review Checklist

Before marking Sprint 1.2 complete:

□ Hero creates curiosity.

□ Story is understandable within one minute.

□ Resume feeling removed.

□ Timeline replaced by narrative chapters.

□ Motion is subtle.

□ Typography feels premium.

□ Reading rhythm feels calm.

□ Footer feels human.

□ Page feels memorable.

□ Every sentence earns its place.

---

# Definition of Done

Sprint is complete only when the homepage no longer feels like a personal portfolio.

It should feel like opening the first page of a beautifully designed memoir.

When a visitor reaches the bottom, they should naturally want to continue into "Becoming."

That curiosity is the success metric.
