# PHASE A — Product Review

> "Great products are not built in one pass.
> They are refined through thoughtful review."

---

# Objective

The goal of Phase A is not to build new features.

The goal is to evaluate the current experience as a first-time visitor.

Every review should answer one question:

> "If I had never met Bibek before, how would this website make me feel?"

---

# Review Philosophy

During this phase, we are no longer developers.

We are no longer writers.

We become editors.

Designers.

Readers.

Visitors.

Every decision should improve clarity, emotion and usability.

---

# Review Process

Every review follows the same workflow.

Implementation

↓

Creative Review

↓

Prioritize Improvements

↓

Implementation Pass

↓

Verification

↓

Approved

# Review Categories

---

## 01 — Storytelling

Question:

Does every page tell a meaningful story?

Checklist

□ Easy to understand

□ Emotionally engaging

□ Natural flow

□ Strong opening

□ Memorable ending

□ No unnecessary repetition

Score

★★★★★

---

## 02 — Reading Experience

Question

Is reading enjoyable?

Checklist

□ Comfortable line length

□ Proper spacing

□ Excellent typography

□ Good rhythm

□ Clear hierarchy

□ Reading progress feels natural

Score

★★★★★

---

## 03 — Visual Hierarchy

Question

□ Clear sections

□ Balanced whitespace

□ Proper emphasis

□ Consistent headings

★★★★★

## 10 — Delight

Question

What made me smile?

This category intentionally has no checklist.

Every review should identify moments that surprise, delight or emotionally connect with the visitor.

These moments make HOME memorable.

---

# Priority Matrix

Every issue discovered during review should receive one priority.

P0 — Critical

Blocks launch.

P1 — Important

Strongly recommended before launch.

P2 — Nice Improvement

Can be completed after launch.

P3 — Future Idea

Document for later.

Creative Review Report

Implementation Tasks

Verification Report

---

# Success Criteria

Phase A is complete when:

✓ Every page tells a clear story.

✓ Every interaction feels intentional.

✓ Navigation is effortless.

✓ Typography disappears into the reading experience.

✓ Visitors remember how HOME made them feel, not how it looked.

At that point...

we move from reviewing the product

to defining its visual identity.
