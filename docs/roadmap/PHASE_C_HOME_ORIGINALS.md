# PHASE C — HOME Originals

> "Don't build features.
>
> Build experiences people remember."

---

# Objective

Every personal website has:

About

Projects

Contact

Resume

HOME should have things that people have never experienced before.

The purpose of HOME Originals is simple:

Give visitors a reason to come back.

---

# Design Philosophy

Every original feature should answer one question:

> "Does this deepen the connection between the visitor and the story?"

If not...

It doesn't belong.

---

# Original 01

## Conversations

Status

★★★★★

Highest Priority

---

### Vision

Visitors shouldn't browse HOME.

They should talk with it.

Not through a chatbot.

Through stories.

Instead of asking AI,

they ask Bibek's journey.

---

### Example

Visitor asks

Why didn't you become an Army Officer?

HOME replies

That's one of the hardest chapters of my life.

Before I answer...

I'd like you to read this memory.

↓

Notebook

↓

Becoming

↓

Related memories

↓

Projects influenced by that lesson

HOME becomes a guide,

not a search engine.

---

### Design Goal

Conversation should feel like:

Sitting with someone over coffee.

Not chatting with AI.

# Original 04

## Story-first Projects

---

Projects shouldn't begin with technology.

Instead they begin with lessons.

Every project answers

What challenge did this solve?

What mistake taught me the most?

What would I do differently today?

Technology becomes supporting information.

Not the headline.

# Original 07

Timeless.

# Principles

Original features should be

Human

Quiet

Useful

Meaningful

Slow

Personal

Avoid

Gamification

Notifications

Popups

Attention-grabbing gimmicks

Complex dashboards

HOME should reward curiosity,

not demand attention.

---

# Long-term Vision

The best compliment HOME could receive isn't

"Beautiful website."

It's

"I stayed much longer than I expected."

or

"I came back just to read one more story."

If visitors return,

HOME has succeeded.
