# EDITORIAL AUDIT
## Version 2 Planning Document

> "Good writing is not adding more.
> It is knowing what deserves to remain."

---

# Purpose

HOME Version 1 is complete.

This document exists before Version 2 begins.

Its purpose is not to redesign HOME.

Its purpose is to protect what already works while carefully improving what doesn't.

Every editorial decision made during Version 2 should reference this document first.

---

# Current State

The foundation of HOME is complete.

The project now includes:

- Homepage
- Becoming
- Notebook Volume 01
- Stories
- Editorial System
- Philosophy
- Creative Brief
- Product Roadmap

The remaining work is no longer technical.

It is editorial.

---

# What We Learned

During our first complete visitor review, one important realization became clear.

HOME is strongest when it remembers.

HOME becomes weaker when it explains.

This insight becomes the guiding principle of Version 2.

---

# Things We Must Protect

These are non-negotiable.

Do not change them unless there is a compelling reason.

## Voice

Honest.

Quiet.

Warm.

Conversational.

Never motivational.

Never performative.

Never exaggerated.

---

## Tone

Speak like a friend.

Not like an author trying to impress.

Not like a speaker giving advice.

Not like a brand.

---

## Pace

Slow.

Comfortable.

Intentional.

Readers should feel invited.

Never rushed.

---

## Simplicity

Short sentences.

Simple words.

Real experiences.

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

---

## Emotional Honesty

Keep failures.

Keep uncertainty.

Keep vulnerability.

Do not polish away difficult moments.

They are part of HOME.

---

## Curiosity

HOME should continue creating questions.

Do not answer everything immediately.

Allow visitors to discover.

---

# What We Improve

Version 2 is refinement.

Not expansion.

Primary focus:

## 1. Repetition

The same life events appear across:

Homepage

Becoming

Notebook

Reduce unnecessary repetition.

Allow each section to contribute something unique.

---

## 2. Story Endings

Current pattern:

Scene

↓

Reflection

↓

Life lesson

This happens too consistently.

Version 2 should introduce variation.

Possible endings:

A memory.

A question.

Dialogue.

Silence.

An unfinished thought.

A reflection.

Not every story needs a moral.

---

## 3. Current Journey

Current presentation feels slightly descriptive.

Goal:

Less "These are my values."

More "This is where I am today."

Visitors should discover the person naturally.

---

## 4. Window Seat

This chapter deserves more emotional presence.

Not necessarily more words.

More space.

More rhythm.

More gravity.

---

## 5. Reading Rhythm

Review:

Whitespace

Paragraph grouping

Typography hierarchy

Visual breathing room

Allow important moments to pause naturally.

---

# What We Will NOT Change

These remain stable.

Do not redesign them.

- Project Philosophy
- Homepage structure
- Becoming chapter order
- Notebook concept
- Overall storytelling direction
- Calm visual identity
- Editorial writing style

---

# Editorial Principles

Every edit should follow these principles.

## Show more.

Explain less.

---

## Trust the reader.

Do not interpret every memory.

Allow visitors to create their own meaning.

---

## Real moments are enough.

Do not manufacture emotion.

Remember honestly.

---

## Silence has value.

Sometimes the strongest ending is no ending.

---

## Specific beats general.

Small memories create larger emotions.

A Nokia phone.

A window seat.

A copied HTML file.

These matter more than abstract advice.

---

## Emotion before explanation.

If a scene already communicates the lesson,

remove the explanation.

---

# Editorial Test

Before accepting any revision, ask:

Does this sound more human?

Does this feel more remembered?

Did we remove unnecessary explanation?

Did we preserve honesty?

Would this still feel true ten years from now?

If the answer is no,

keep editing.

---

# Version 2 Mission

Version 2 is not about writing more.

It is about writing better.

Less explanation.

More trust.

Less polishing.

More truth.

The goal is simple:

Help readers feel like they are listening to someone's life,

not reading someone's content.

---

# Success

Version 2 succeeds if readers leave remembering moments,

not messages.

They should remember:

The Nokia phone.

Three failed attempts.

Copying HTML.

Code Pros.

The Window Seat.

Dinner with family.

Not because we explained why they mattered.

Because they already did.
