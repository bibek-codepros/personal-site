# HOME
## Project Philosophy

> "Every journey begins somewhere.
> Mine began with curiosity."

---

# Why HOME Exists

HOME was never meant to be another personal website.

It wasn't created to impress recruiters.

It wasn't created to collect clients.

It wasn't created to become a portfolio.

HOME exists because stories matter.

Throughout life, I realised that the moments which shaped me most were rarely the biggest achievements.

They were quiet conversations.

Unexpected failures.

People who believed in me.

Dreams that changed direction.

Small decisions that slowly became turning points.

Those moments deserve a place to live.

HOME is that place.

---

# What HOME Is

HOME is a living collection of memories.

It is where my experiences become stories.

My lessons become reflections.

My work becomes part of a larger journey.

Visitors are not expected to admire my career.

They're invited to understand the person behind it.

---

# What HOME Is Not

HOME is not a portfolio template.

HOME is not a résumé.

HOME is not a startup landing page.

HOME is not a social media feed.

HOME is not built around achievements.

Achievements may appear here.

But they are never the point.

The story always comes first.

---

# What Success Means

Traffic is not success.

Followers are not success.

Virality is not success.

HOME succeeds when someone leaves saying:

"I felt something."

If one person finds comfort.

If one person smiles.

If one person feels less alone.

If one person chooses to keep going because of something they read here...

HOME has already achieved its purpose.

---

# Design Principles

Every decision should make HOME feel:

Warm.

Honest.

Calm.

Thoughtful.

Human.

Timeless.

If something feels noisy, flashy or distracting,

it probably doesn't belong.

---

# Writing Principles

Write honestly.

Write simply.

Write only what is true.

Don't exaggerate.

Don't perform.

Don't write to impress.

Write as if you're speaking to one person over coffee.

Leave space for readers to find their own meaning.

---

# Product Principles

Every feature must strengthen the connection between the visitor and the story.

Before building anything, ask:

> Does this help someone understand the person behind the work?

If the answer is no,

don't build it.

Complexity is never the goal.

Clarity is.

---

# What Never Changes

No matter how much HOME evolves:

Stories come before technology.

People come before metrics.

Meaning comes before aesthetics.

Curiosity comes before certainty.

HOME should always feel like somewhere people are welcome to stay.

Never somewhere they are rushed through.

---

# Looking Forward

HOME is intentionally unfinished.

Life continues.

New experiences will become new stories.

New stories will become new notebook volumes.

Some pages will grow.

Others will quietly stay exactly as they are.

That is part of the design.

HOME should evolve naturally,

just as people do.

---

# A Note to Future Me

If you're reading this years from now—

remember why you started.

Don't chase trends.

Don't add features because everyone else has them.

Don't turn HOME into something louder than it needs to be.

Protect the quiet moments.

Protect the honesty.

Protect the curiosity.

Those are the foundations of this project.

Everything else can change.

Those should not.

---

*The goal was to build a place worth coming back to."*
