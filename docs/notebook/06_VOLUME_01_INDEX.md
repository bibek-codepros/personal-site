# Volume 1

## Where It All Started

Status: In Progress

Target Notes: 20

Purpose:

The first published collection inside HOME.

Volume 1 introduces the person behind the website—not through achievements, but through memories that quietly shaped who he became.

Unlike **Becoming**, these stories are not arranged as one continuous narrative.

Each note can be read independently.

Together, they form a portrait.

---

# Editorial Theme

Curiosity.

Unexpected turns.

Quiet failures.

Meaningful people.

Small victories.

The beginning of a lifelong journey.

Readers should finish Volume 1 feeling that they know the person—not just the career.
