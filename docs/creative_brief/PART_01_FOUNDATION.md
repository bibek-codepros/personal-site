# PART 01
# FOUNDATION

> "Before understanding how HOME should look,
> understand why HOME exists."

---

# Why HOME Exists

HOME was never created because I needed a website.

It was created because I needed a place.

A place where memories could live.

A place where lessons wouldn't disappear.

A place where my younger self, my present self and my future self could quietly meet.

Throughout life I've realised something.

The biggest moments are rarely the ones that change us.

It's usually the small moments.

A teacher who believed in you.

A dream that quietly disappeared.

A conversation over coffee.

A first flight.

A difficult goodbye.

An unexpected opportunity.

Those moments deserve more than disappearing inside photo albums or social media timelines.

HOME gives them permanence.

---

# What HOME Is

HOME is...

A digital home.

A living notebook.

A quiet autobiography.

A conversation.

A reflection.

A memory.

A journey still being written.

Visitors should never feel they're consuming content.

They should feel they're spending time with another human being.

---

# What HOME Is Not

HOME is not LinkedIn.

HOME is not Medium.

HOME is not Instagram.

HOME is not a portfolio template.

HOME is not an online CV.

HOME is not a startup landing page.

HOME is not a collection of achievements.

Those things already exist.

HOME exists because those things are not enough.

---

# The Soul Of HOME

Every meaningful project has something invisible.

A soul.

HOME's soul is curiosity.

Curiosity led me toward computers.

Curiosity led me away from the Army.

Curiosity made me learn HTML.

Curiosity made me become a leader.

Curiosity made me travel.

Curiosity made me write.

If curiosity ever disappears from HOME,

HOME stops being HOME.

Protect it.

---

# Honesty Above Everything

Never exaggerate.

Never manufacture emotion.

Never write something because it sounds impressive.

If a story happened quietly,

let it remain quiet.

Truth is always more interesting than performance.

Readers recognise honesty immediately.

That trust is the most valuable thing HOME owns.

Never sacrifice it.

---

# The Reader's Journey

Visitors should experience HOME like this.

Curiosity.

↓

Comfort.

↓

Understanding.

↓

Reflection.

↓

Connection.

↓

Hope.

That emotional progression matters more than navigation.

Every page contributes to it.

---

# Technology Is Supporting Cast

React is not HOME.

Next.js is not HOME.

Animations are not HOME.

Typography is not HOME.

Photography is not HOME.

They exist for one purpose.

To help stories disappear into the reader's imagination.

Technology should never ask for attention.

Stories deserve it.

---

# Simplicity

Whenever possible,

remove.

Simplify.

Reduce.

The best design decision is often subtraction.

Whitespace is content.

Silence is content.

Stillness is content.

Do not fear empty space.

Readers need room to think.

---

# Human Before Professional

Many personal websites begin with careers.

HOME begins with humanity.

Profession comes later.

Work matters.

Projects matter.

Experience matters.

But none of those explain who someone is.

People do.

Memories do.

Relationships do.

HOME always introduces the human before the professional.

---

# Long-Term Thinking

HOME is not Version 1.

HOME is not Version 2.

HOME is a lifelong project.

Volumes will continue.

Stories will continue.

Lessons will continue.

Growth will continue.

Never build features that assume HOME is finished.

Because it never will be.

---

# The Final Test

Before approving any change,

ask yourself one question.

If someone visited HOME ten years from today,

would this decision still feel right?

If the answer is yes,

you're probably protecting the project.

If the answer depends on trends,

you're probably following them.

HOME should outlive trends.

It should grow with life.
