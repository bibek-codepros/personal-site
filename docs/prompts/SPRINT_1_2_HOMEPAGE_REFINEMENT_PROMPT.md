# Primary Objective

Transform the homepage from a high-quality portfolio into an immersive narrative experience.

A visitor should feel like they are entering someone's world—not browsing a personal website.

The homepage should naturally encourage visitors to continue into the **Becoming** section.

---

# Core Principles

The homepage should feel:

- calm
- intentional
- elegant
- personal
- premium

Avoid anything flashy, trendy, or overly decorative.

Whitespace is a feature.

Typography is part of the storytelling.

Motion should support reading, never compete with it.

# Design Expectations

Typography should resemble a modern memoir.

Animations should feel invisible.

Spacing should create breathing room.

Every section should have a clear visual hierarchy.

Avoid long uninterrupted walls of text.

Alternate large and small visual moments.

The homepage should feel emotionally paced.

---

# Things You MUST NOT Do

Do not rewrite the manuscript.

Do not summarize the story.

Do not introduce AI-generated wording.

Do not replace the narrative voice.

Do not add generic marketing language.

Do not turn HOME into a portfolio.

Do not make it look like a startup landing page.

---

# Engineering Expectations

Maintain performance.

Keep Lighthouse scores high.

Support reduced-motion preferences.

Ensure accessibility.

Use reusable components where appropriate.

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

---

# Internal Review

Before considering Sprint 1.2 complete, perform your own design review.

Ask yourself:

- Does the homepage create curiosity?
- Does it feel like opening a memoir?
- Would a first-time visitor naturally continue reading?
- Does every section have a purpose?
- Is there any section that still feels like LinkedIn or a résumé?

If the answer to any of these is "yes", continue refining before stopping.
