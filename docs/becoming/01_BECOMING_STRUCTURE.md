# BECOMING STRUCTURE

Status: Production Ready

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Becoming experience is the heart of HOME.

It is not a webpage.

It is not a blog.

It is not a portfolio.

It is the digital equivalent of opening a beautifully printed memoir.

The visitor should feel like they are sitting down with a cup of coffee while someone quietly tells them their story.

The objective is to remove every unnecessary distraction and allow the story to become the primary interface.

---

# Experience Philosophy

Homepage introduces the person.

Becoming introduces the journey.

Everything should slow down.

Visitors should forget they are browsing a website.

They should simply read.

---

# Reading Principles

Every design decision must support reading.

Every interaction must support emotion.

Every transition must support curiosity.

Nothing should interrupt the narrative.

# Opening Transition

The transition from Homepage to Becoming should immediately change the atmosphere.

Instead of immediately loading content:

Fade the homepage.

Display a full-screen transition.

Show one sentence.

Pause.

Fade.

Show another sentence.

Pause.

Fade.

Display:

HOME

Chapter One

Where It All Began

Then begin reading.

The transition should take approximately 3–5 seconds.

It should feel cinematic but never slow.

---

# Chapter Structure

Every chapter follows the same structure.

Consistency creates comfort.

---

Chapter Header

Chapter Number

Chapter Title

Short Chapter Description

Estimated Reading Time

---

Reading Body

Render the markdown file exactly as written.

Do not rewrite.

Do not summarize.

Respect paragraph spacing.

Respect quotes.

Respect pauses.

---

Supporting Visuals

Maximum:

One hero illustration.

Maximum two supporting memory images.

Images should support memories.

Never interrupt reading.

---

Reflection

At the end of each chapter include one highlighted sentence.

This acts as the emotional takeaway.

Example:

"Sometimes failure isn't the opposite of progress.

Sometimes it is progress."

This should come from the manuscript whenever possible.

Do not invent unnecessary quotes.

---

Chapter Ending

Every chapter ends with a quiet transition.

Never immediately jump to the next chapter.

Display:

You have reached the end of Chapter One.

Take a breath.

Continue when you're ready.

↓

Button

Open Chapter Two →

---

Chapter Navigation

Minimal.

Elegant.

Previous

Current Chapter

Next

No large navigation menus.

No sidebar.

No table of contents floating on screen.

Reading always comes first.

---

Progress Indicator

Do not use a progress bar.

Instead display:

01 • 02 • 03 • 04 • 05 • 06

Highlight the current chapter.

Keep it small.

Keep it elegant.

---

Reading Width

Maximum content width:

680px

Reading should resemble a premium printed book.

---

Typography

Large chapter titles.

Comfortable body text.

Generous paragraph spacing.

Readable line height.

Quotes receive their own visual treatment.

No justified text.

---

Quotes

Quotes should become moments.

Large.

Centered.

Surrounded by whitespace.

Never enclosed in quotation boxes.

Never decorated.

Words alone create emphasis.

---

Images

Images should appear naturally within the story.

Avoid predictable alternating layouts.

Instead place them where they emotionally belong.

Examples:

After a childhood memory.

After the first airplane story.

After joining Code Pros.

Images should reinforce memory rather than decorate content.

---

Illustrations

Illustrations should remain minimal.

Thin stroke.

Editorial.

Purposeful.

Each chapter receives one illustration representing its emotional theme.

---

Scrolling Experience

Scrolling should feel calm.

No aggressive animations.

No forced interactions.

Allow visitors to pause naturally.

Never rush them.

---

Ambient Background

Each chapter receives a slightly different background tone.

Chapter One

Warm White

Chapter Two

Very Soft Green

Chapter Three

Soft Neutral Grey

Chapter Four

Light Blue

Chapter Five

Very Light Sky

Chapter Six

Warm Beige

The change should be almost invisible.

Felt more than noticed.

---

Chapter Connections

Each chapter should naturally introduce the next.

Never feel disconnected.

The visitor should feel like reading one continuous memoir rather than separate pages.

---

Closing Experience

After Chapter Six:

Do not redirect immediately.

Display:

You have reached the end.

But not the end of the journey.

Thank you for spending time inside HOME.

Pause.

Then provide two options.

Return Home

Emotional Goal

At the end of Becoming, visitors should not think:

"I learned about Bibek."

Instead they should think:

"I understand him a little better."

That difference defines the success of this experience.

---

Definition of Done

The Becoming experience is complete only when:

□ Reading feels effortless.

□ Navigation feels invisible.

□ Chapters feel connected.

□ The interface never competes with the story.

□ Visitors naturally continue reading without being instructed to do so.

□ The experience feels closer to reading a beautifully designed memoir than browsing a modern website.
