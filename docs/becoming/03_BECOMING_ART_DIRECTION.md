# BECOMING ART DIRECTION

Status: Production Ready

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the visual language of the Becoming experience.

Becoming is not another page.

It is the reading room of HOME.

The visitor should forget they are on a website.

The interface should disappear.

Only the story should remain.

---

# Core Philosophy

If Homepage feels like someone inviting you inside...

Becoming feels like sitting beside them while they tell you their story.

Everything becomes quieter.

Everything becomes slower.

Everything becomes more intimate.

---

# Emotional Keywords

The Becoming experience should feel:

• Honest

• Quiet

• Reflective

• Personal

• Warm

• Timeless

• Comfortable

• Human

• Hopeful

Avoid:

• Corporate

• Trendy

• Flashy

• Over-designed

• Decorative

---

# Visual References

Primary references

Apple Editorial

Kinfolk Magazine

Aesop Journal

Read.cv

The Gentleman's Journal

Medium (only reading width)

Not a blog.

Not a startup website.

---

# Background

Background should remain soft.

No gradients.

No textures.

No patterns.

Each chapter receives a barely noticeable tone shift.

Example:

Chapter One

Warm White

Chapter Two

Soft Olive Tint

Chapter Three

Cool Neutral

Chapter Four

Pale Blue

Chapter Five

Sky White

Chapter Six

Warm Cream

Visitors should feel the transition rather than notice it.

---

# Typography

Typography becomes the primary visual element.

Large chapter titles.

Comfortable reading size.

Generous spacing.

Paragraphs should resemble printed books.

Reading should never feel dense.

---

# Reading Width

Maximum width:

680px

Never exceed 720px.

The eyes should travel comfortably.

---

# Chapter Header

Every chapter begins with:

HOME

↓

Chapter Number

↓

Chapter Title

↓

Subtitle

↓

Estimated Reading Time

↓

Small divider

↓

Story

This rhythm never changes.

Consistency builds familiarity.

---

# White Space

Whitespace is intentional.

Never reduce spacing simply to fit more content.

Large spaces allow emotional moments to breathe.

Reading should feel relaxed.

---

# Paragraph Rhythm

Alternate paragraph lengths.

Mix:

Short observations.

Medium paragraphs.

Occasional long reflections.

Avoid large uninterrupted blocks.

Every few paragraphs should feel like a natural pause.

---

# Quotes

Quotes are emotional anchors.

Presentation:

Centered.

Large.

Italic.

No quotation box.

No background color.

No border.

Only words.

Whitespace above and below should be generous.

Visitors should naturally pause.

---

# Images

Images support memory.

They never interrupt it.

Each chapter should contain:

One hero image or illustration.

Maximum two supporting images.

Preferred placement:

Immediately after emotionally significant memories.

Avoid predictable left-right layouts.

Images should feel discovered.

---

# Photography Direction

Photography should feel documentary.

Natural light.

Real places.

No stock photography.

No staged portraits.

Future photography examples:

Childhood objects

Old Nokia phone

Notebook

Coffee

Army preparation

Laptop

Airport

Window seat

Bali

Code Pros

Family moments

Simple everyday life

Nothing should feel overly produced.

---

# Illustration Style

Minimal line illustrations.

Thin strokes.

Editorial quality.

Soft rounded edges.

Purposeful.

Each illustration represents one memory.

Examples:

Old Nokia phone

Army cap

HTML brackets

Notebook

Compass

Airplane window

Coffee mug

Open book

Avoid generic business illustrations.

---

# Icons

Use Lucide Icons.

Consistent size.

Outline only.

No filled icons.

Icons should explain.

Never decorate.

Suggested mapping:

Curiosity → Search

Dream → Medal

Starting Again → Sprout

Learning → Book

Leadership → Compass

Journey → Plane

Reflection → Coffee

Notebook → Book Open

Conversation → Message Circle

---

# Motion

Motion should almost disappear.

Preferred animations:

Opacity

Small translateY

Gentle blur

Slow image reveal

Soft fade

Avoid:

Bounce

Scale

Rotation

Elastic

Overshoot

Every animation must support reading.

---

# Scroll Experience

Scrolling should feel like turning pages.

No sudden visual interruptions.

Allow long quiet sections.

Visitors should naturally slow down.

---

# Chapter Transition

End Chapter

↓

Whitespace

↓

Reflection

↓

Pause

↓

Continue Button

↓

Fade

↓

Next Chapter

Transitions should feel cinematic.

Never abrupt.

---

# Reflection Sections

Every chapter ends with one emotional takeaway.

This section becomes visually different.

Large typography.

Centered.

Quiet.

Minimal.

No illustration.

No decoration.

---

# Small Details

Reward attentive readers.

Examples:

Small location captions.

Tiny year markers.

Subtle notebook-style labels.

Airport code.

Coffee origin.

Date.

These should feel like little discoveries.

Never dominate.

---

# Ambient Details

Optional additions:

Soft grain texture (extremely subtle)

Tiny divider lines

Book chapter numbering

Small page numbers

Elegant separators

These details should make the experience feel crafted.

---

# Accessibility

Maintain excellent readability.

Large line height.

Strong contrast.

Keyboard navigation.

Reduced motion support.

Readable on mobile.

Comfortable on tablets.

---

# Mobile Experience

Mobile should feel like reading a pocket book.

Margins increase slightly.

Images become full width.

Typography scales carefully.

Nothing should feel compressed.

---

# Emotional Goal

At the end of each chapter, visitors should quietly pause before continuing.

They should never feel rushed.

The interface should encourage reflection.

Not consumption.

---

# Success Metric

The Becoming experience is successful when a visitor forgets they are scrolling through a website.

Instead, they should feel like someone trusted them enough to share a part of their life.

That feeling is the true visual identity of HOME.
