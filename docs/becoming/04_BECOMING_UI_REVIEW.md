# BECOMING UI REVIEW

Status: Creative Director Review

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document is the final quality gate before the Becoming experience is considered complete.

It is not an implementation guide.

It is a creative review.

Every point below should be reviewed before the chapter experience is approved.

Do not stop after fixing obvious issues.

Keep refining until the interface disappears and only the story remains.

---

# SECTION 01

## First Impression

★★★★★

The visitor should immediately feel they have entered a different world from the homepage.

Questions:

• Does Becoming immediately feel quieter?

• Does it feel more intimate?

• Does it invite reading?

If not,

continue refining.

---

# SECTION 02

## Opening Transition

★★★★★

The transition from Homepage must feel intentional.

Review:

Transition timing

Fade quality

Typography

Whitespace

Sentence pacing

The visitor should naturally slow down before Chapter One begins.

Typography

# SECTION 05

# SECTION 06

## Reading Rhythm

★★★★★

Read the entire chapter.

Ignore the interface.

Ask:

Does the reading naturally flow?

Are there moments to pause?

Does the page breathe?

If reading feels rushed,

increase whitespace.

---

# SECTION 07

## Quotes

★★★★★

Quotes should become emotional anchors.

Review:

Typography

Whitespace

Position

Contrast

They should feel memorable.

Never decorative.

---

# SECTION 08

## Images

★★★★★

Images support memories.

Review every image.

Ask:

Does this image strengthen the story?

Or simply decorate it?

If decorative,

remove it.

Scale

# SECTION 21

## Story First

★★★★★

Hide every illustration.

Hide every icon.

Hide every animation.

Read only the words.

Does the chapter still work?

If yes,

the story is strong.

Now restore the visuals.

Do they improve the experience?

Or simply decorate it?

---

# SECTION 22

## Emotional Audit

★★★★★

Ask yourself:

Would I continue reading?

Would I share this chapter?

Would I remember this tomorrow?

Would I feel something?

If not,

continue refining.

---

# SECTION 23

## Creative Director Questions

Before approving Becoming, answer:

Does it feel handcrafted?

Does it feel timeless?

Does it respect the reader?

Does it encourage reflection?

Does it avoid trends?

Would Apple over-design this?

Would Kinfolk simplify it?

Would Aesop remove something?

If the answer to any of these is "no",

continue refining.

---

# SECTION 24

## Final Approval Checklist

The Becoming experience is approved only when:

□ Homepage and Becoming feel connected.

□ The interface disappears.

□ Reading becomes effortless.

□ Images support memory.

□ Motion supports emotion.

□ Navigation becomes invisible.

□ Typography feels editorial.

□ Visitors naturally continue.

□ Every chapter feels like a real chapter in a memoir.

□ HOME no longer feels like a website.

It feels like a book.

---

# Definition of Done

The Becoming experience succeeds when visitors stop thinking about the design entirely.

Instead, they quietly begin thinking about their own life while reading someone else's.

That is the highest compliment HOME can receive.
